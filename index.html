RULES

The shotgun is loaded with a hidden mix of live and blank shells. Shoot yourself or the dealer. A live shell costs one heart. A blank fired at yourself gives you another turn. Empty the dealer's hearts before he empties yours.

Controls: tap the dealer, or SHOOT DEALER, to fire at him. Tap anywhere in the bottom third of the table, or SHOOT YOU, to fire at yourself.
